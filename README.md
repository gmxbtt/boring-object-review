# The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line

Entry for make.fun's **"The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line"** bounty.

## The review

> The rubber band: spends years holding your junk together in a drawer, then snaps the one time you actually need it, and the pieces vanish into the drawer forever.

162 characters, one line.

## Why the rubber band?

It is the most boring object imaginable: a loop of vulcanised rubber whose
entire job is to sit still and hold things together. Nobody buys one on
purpose; they arrive free, in the post, around vegetables, and accumulate in
a drawer until you own hundreds and can never find one when it matters.

## Why the line is true — and funny

- Rubber bands really do dry out, perish and snap without warning — usually
  at the exact moment you stretch one around something important.
- The snapped pieces really do disappear: too small to find, too useless to
  chase, they join the permanent sediment of the junk drawer.
- The joke lands because the object's one virtue (holding things together)
  is also its one failure mode (snapping), and it fails on your schedule,
  not its own.
